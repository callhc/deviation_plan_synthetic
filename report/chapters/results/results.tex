\subsubsection{Use cases}

This section highlights meaningful and realistic use cases where the simulation tool could be
applied. These scenarios are particularly relevant in the context of planned road closures and the
design of deviation plans. By simulating these situations, the tool can help anticipate traffic
behavior. Below are several examples, with a focus on the Brussels region, that illustrate the
potential of such simulations for urban traffic management:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Roadworks at Carrefour Léonard}: The Carrefour Léonard is a well-known place where
big roadworks are happening. Simulating such a scenario could be highly valuable as the tool could
be used to compare multiple deviation strategies and identify the one that best reduces congestion.
\item \textbf{Closure of a major tunnel}: Tunnel closures are not uncommon in Brussels, whether due
to maintenance or unexpected incidents. While the immediate effects on adjacent streets are often
visible the simulation tool could provide insights into other consequences, such as congestion in
distant neighborhoods. This is especially relevant in the age of GPS navigation systems where
drivers may be rerouted through less obvious paths, potentially overloading streets not originally
designed for heavy traffic.
\item \textbf{Closure of a neighborhood}: The complete closure of a neighborhood (for a marathon,
festival...) presents complex traffic challenges. Not only does it block local roads but it also
changes the flow of vehicles in the surrounding areas. With the simulation tool, traffic planners
could evaluate different deviation strategies to minimize congestion.
\end{enumerate}
